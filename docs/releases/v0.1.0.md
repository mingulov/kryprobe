# KryProbe v0.1.0

Prepared for the first public release. The version in Cargo is 0.1.0;
publication and the immutable `v0.1.0` tag are separate release actions.

KryProbe observes use of the Linux kernel crypto API and reports its
coverage and losses. The first release includes system-wide
`watch`, `report` and `check`, the api-returns and request-lifecycle
profiles, validated session replay, and digest-bound binary/BPF packaging.
It also includes the QEMU demo harness and its recorded limitations.
See the [quick start](../kcrypto-quickstart.md) and
[support table](../kcrypto-support.md) for supported boundaries.

## Supported scope

- Linux x86-64: api-returns on capable 6.12+ kernels; request-lifecycle
  on capable 7.x kernels, with a typed refusal on the 6.12 floor.
  Runtime probes determine availability.
- API-return counts establish observed calls, not provider-body execution,
  delivered operations or async completion. Lifecycle terminals require
  their corresponding observation coverage; unknowns stay explicit.
- The early-boot demo covers initramfs-local observation before controlled
  encrypted-volume unlock: three fresh boots and late/broken-observer
  negative controls. Pre-attach activity is unobserved. Continuous capture
  through `switch_root` into the installed system is deferred.
- Virtual-device demo claims stop at driver selection. Queue/offload proof,
  transparent failover and physical-accelerator claims remain excluded.

## Measured performance limits

The owner accepts these limits for this first release. Full item-2
performance qualification and the broader R1 milestone remain incomplete.
No general <=5% throughput-loss or <=10% tail-latency-increase guarantee
is made.

The 7.x 4 KiB aggregation workloads at roughly 41-47k operations/second
incurred about 18-20% lower throughput and 20-22% higher workload p99.
Both fail their frozen budgets. The 1 MiB/7.0.14 result remains
INCONCLUSIVE; the 1 MiB/7.2.6 result passes its workload-specific budget.
All 13 detailed-mode sets remain INVALID because of truncation or counted
loss; they do not provide qualified overhead ratios.

A single owner-authorized offline checker correction reevaluated the
existing Linux 6.12.111 recordings. No new guests or samples were used:

| Floor workload | Original | Corrected | Throughput ratio | Workload p99 ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 KiB aggregate | INVALID, 0/5 pairs | ENVELOPE, 5/5 pairs | 0.739196 | 1.394026 |
| 1 MiB aggregate | INVALID, 0/5 pairs | ENVELOPE, 5/5 pairs | 0.889887 | 1.243607 |

Ratios are observed/baseline medians. ENVELOPE means measured operating
data for sets with no predefined budget, not a budget PASS. The correction
removes an erroneous destroy-skip rejection while preserving the recorded
counter value 3 and every measured value. The other 24 sets and all four
diagnostics are unchanged. The campaign still reports INVALID, with 14
reasons (13 detail sets and one stack diagnostic).

Existing measurement gaps also remain: no numerical drain-lag bound,
unreported map/state occupancy and stop cost, incomplete recorded host
conditions, and no same-kernel bulk perturbation control for the floor.
The [release ledger](../release-ledger.md) preserves the other carried
limits and original evidence identities.

## Demo evidence and scheduling

The owner explicitly permits kernel stack addresses in the original
evidence from the completed disposable p10a4/p10a7 demo guests. Original
files and checksums are retained. This exception covers those campaigns;
it does not authorize publishing secrets or arbitrary production captures.
The optional redaction recipe was not selected for this release.

Future QEMU demo runs require the task/common locks and
`--refuse-foreign`. They wait or refuse if another guest is running and
never stop unrelated guests. This is a demo-lab scheduling rule.

## Installation and source

The prepared Linux x86-64 bundle contains `bin/`, the v2 manifest,
payload checksums, `packaging/install.sh`, notices and provenance.
This measured binary requires glibc 2.39 or newer and `libgcc_s.so.1`;
its ELF symbol requirements are recorded in the package provenance.
From its extracted directory:

```sh
sha256sum -c sha256sums.txt
sudo sh packaging/install.sh --stage "$PWD"
kryprobe doctor --versions
kryprobe doctor
```

The corresponding source archive accompanies the binary. Build from source
with the pinned tools in [the dependency pins](../dependencies/pins.md)
using `packaging/build-release.sh --dest /owned/empty-stage`.
Host tests and artifact checks do not replace live qualification.
